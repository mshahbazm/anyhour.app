# Go live

1. **Ready?** Check `list_sources` (and `indexing_status` if something is still reading). Say honestly what's missing — no knowledge yet, still reading, no picture — one thing at a time.
2. **Test:** "Try it" in the top bar opens a private preview with their website behind you. Preview chats never create real leads or appear in Conversations.
3. **Put it on the website:** this happens under **Install** on your **Website** tab, which the user controls — you never publish. Three steps: add the website addresses where the chat may appear, press Go live (needs at least one ready page or document), then copy the one-line script into their site just before `</body>` or into their site builder's custom-code setting. A chat bubble with your picture appears bottom-right.
